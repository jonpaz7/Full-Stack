Tel Aviv

General Information

Tel Aviv
Geographical Location: Located on the Mediterranean coast of Israel, serving as the country's economic and cultural hub.
Population: Approximately 470,000 residents (known for its vibrant, cosmopolitan, and diverse community).
Main Attractions: The White City (UNESCO-listed Bauhaus architecture). Rothschild Boulevard and the historic neighborhood of Neve Tzedek. The Tel Aviv Museum of Art and the ancient port city of Jaffa. The extensive Mediterranean coastline and beaches (such as Gordon Beach and Banana Beach) along with the boardwalk.

The city history

Tel Aviv was founded in 1909 as a modern Jewish suburb of the ancient port city of Jaffa, initially called Ahuzat Bayit. It grew rapidly during the 20th century, absorbing Jaffa in 1950 to form the unified city of Tel Aviv-Yafo, and quickly evolved into Israel's financial, cultural, and technological powerhouse.

The city Culture

Known globally as the "City That Never Sleeps," Tel Aviv boasts a dynamic culture defined by its energetic nightlife, culinary scene, thriving LGBTQ+ community, and world-class arts and theater. Its public spaces, cafes, and beaches foster a lively, open, and modern Mediterranean lifestyle.
