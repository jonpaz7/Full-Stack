Echoes of Silence

The movie

Movie Name: Echoes of Silence
Director: Elena Vance
Main Cast: Julian Thorne, Clara Reed, and Marcus Vane
Release Date: May 20, 2027

The plot

Summary: In a future where human memories can be digitized and traded, Julian Thorne, a "Memory Architect," discovers a recurring glitch in his own past. As he peels back the layers of his stored experiences, he realizes that his entire life has been a curated fiction designed to hide a planetary-scale environmental collapse. He must race against time to reclaim his authentic memories before the system erases him permanently.

The Characters

Julian Thorne: A brilliant architect of digital dreams who starts to doubt the reality of his own existence.
Clara Reed: A rogue archivist who helps Julian navigate the dangerous underbelly of the memory black market.
Marcus Vane: The ruthless CEO of the corporation that owns the world's data stream and enforces the memory laws.
