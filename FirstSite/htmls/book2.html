The Great Gatsby

General Information

The Great Gatsby - F. Scott Fitzgerald

Plot Summary

  • The Jazz Age Setting: Set in the summer of 1922 on Long Island, the novel captures the decadence, prosperity, and social upheaval of the "Roaring Twenties" in America.
  • The Protagonist's Obsession: The story follows the enigmatic millionaire Jay Gatsby and his relentless, tragic pursuit to reunite with his former lover, the beautiful and wealthy Daisy Buchanan.
  • The Central Conflict: Narrated by Nick Carraway, the novel explores the tension between "old money" (East Egg) and "new money" (West Egg), highlighting the corruption and moral decay hidden beneath the surface of lavish wealth.
  • Symbolism and Themes: Key symbols like the green light at the end of Daisy’s dock and the desolate "Valley of Ashes" represent the unattainable nature of the American Dream and the emptiness of materialism.
  • The Tragic Conclusion: The narrative culminates in a series of devastating events, revealing the consequences of reckless ambition and the inability to repeat the past.
