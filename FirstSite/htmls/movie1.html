Shadow of Truth

The movie

Movie Name: Shadow of Truth
Director: Daniel Cohen
Main Cast: Tomer Lev, Sarah Ben-David, and Michael Alon
Release Date: November 12, 2026

The plot

Summary: The story follows Tomer Lev, a brilliant but alienated forensic investigator who loses faith in the legal system after his best friend is falsely accused. His life takes a shocking turn when he investigates the seemingly routine suicide of a senior politician and discovers hidden evidence proving it was actually a sophisticated murder. Suddenly, all digital and physical evidence at the scene begins to alter itself to match the official suicide narrative, plunging Tomer into a dangerous game of survival and paranoia.

The Characters

Tomer Lev: The protagonist and forensic expert whose pursuit of the truth puts him at the center of a massive conspiracy.
Sarah Ben-David: An investigative journalist who allies with Tomer, risking her career and life to expose the corruption.
Michael Alon: A shadowy and powerful government official orchestrating the cover-up behind the scenes.
