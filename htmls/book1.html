In Search of Lost Time - Jonathan Shahar

Plot Summary

  • The Core Technology: Scientists invent "Echo-Sound," a revolutionary device capable of capturing and playing back ambient audio waves lingering in the air from centuries prior. Ethical Dilemmas: The narrative explores the unintended consequences of total transparency and whether some secrets are better left buried in the past.
  • Uncovering Secrets: Characters can listen to anything from pivotal historical events to forgotten, private conversations, driving the mystery forward.
  • The Central Conflict: Privacy disappears overnight, as no past conversation is truly safe from being overheard, leading to black markets for historical audio and social upheaval.
  • Personal & Historical Stakes: The protagonist uses Echo-Sound to solve a specific cold case, personal family mystery, or major historical conspiracy.
